# Rental market source assessment

## Source and reproducibility

The analysis uses the historical Apartment for Rent Classified collection from the UCI Machine Learning Repository (2019). Source DOI: [https://doi.org/10.24432/C5X623](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5X623). The dataset is distributed under CC BY 4.0 and is credited to its original repository. | Stage | Listings |

| After exact-row deduplication | 99,403 |
| Distinct eligible listings with city and state | 99,101 |

The 99,487 initial eligible records are positive-rent, positive-area, USD-denominated monthly classified listings. Removing exact row duplicates produces 99,403 provisional distinct observations, 99,101 of which include both city and state. These are **exploratory cohorts**, not a finalized model-training dataset. Duplicate removal is based only on exact complete-row identity. No price or area outlier has been removed at this stage.

## Preliminary market portrait

The provisional distinct cohort has median advertised monthly rent $1,350, an interquartile range of $1,014–$1,795, and a 99th percentile of $4,250. These are asking rents, not signed leases or current market prices. Location comparisons are descriptive and not adjusted for unit attributes or geographical sampling imbalances.

The source contains 99,492 rows. The provisional city-and-state cohort covers 51 state codes and 2,978 city names. State-level outputs summarize all represented groups; the public city table shows locations with at least 30 eligible distinct listings to avoid unstable tiny-group comparisons.

## Interpretation guardrails

- Geographic differences are descriptive and must not be described as causal regional premiums.
- Amenities and pet policy contain substantial missingness. Missing values cannot be equated to absence.
- Records may contain reposts, duplicate units under different IDs, and nonrepresentative source coverage.
- Listing dates describe historical classified records and do not establish present-day rents.
- `price_display` directly restates the outcome and must not be used as a price-prediction feature.
- Figures depicting prices or square footage may restrict displayed ranges to the 99th percentile; those display limits do not remove records from tables.
- Raw addresses, free text, and individual listing records are not exported to the public summary.

## Artifacts

The `tables` folder contains source quality, cohort flow, quantiles, state and city summaries, bedroom and area comparisons, and correlations. The `figures` folder contains eight research charts. All are generated by `python -m market_study.market_evidence` from the original uncommitted CSV.
